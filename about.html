About JIJAMATA PUBLIC SCHOOL

Established in 2003, Jijamata Public School has been a beacon of educational excellence in Bhenda BK, Ahmednagar, Maharashtra for over two decades. Our institution stands as a testament to quality school education, character building, and the holistic development of young minds from LKG to 10th Standard.

Affiliated to the Maharashtra State Board (MSBSHSE), we believe in nurturing not just academic brilliance but also moral values, creativity, and leadership qualities that prepare our students for the challenges of tomorrow. Our commitment to excellence has earned us recognition as a trusted and respected school in the region.

Vision & Mission

Our guiding principles and commitment to quality school education

Our Vision

To be a leading school recognised for academic excellence, holistic student development, and strong values — nurturing responsible, confident, and caring citizens who contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

  • Provide quality, inclusive education from LKG to 10th Standard
  • Foster curiosity, creativity, and critical thinking in every student
  • Build strong moral values, discipline, and social responsibility
  • Encourage sports, arts, and co-curricular participation
  • Empower every child to achieve their fullest potential

Principal's Message

Welcome to Jijamata Public School. Education is not merely the transfer of knowledge — it is the shaping of a child's character, confidence, and compassion. At Jijamata, we believe every child is gifted, and our role is to discover and nurture that gift.

Our school is dedicated to providing a safe, stimulating, and caring environment where students from LKG to 10th Standard can grow academically, emotionally, and socially. We are committed to blending traditional values with modern teaching methods.

With our dedicated teaching staff, well-equipped facilities, and a strong focus on both academics and co-curricular activities, we strive every day to make learning a joyful and meaningful journey for every child entrusted to our care.

Balasaheb Ugalmugale

Principal, Jijamata Public School
