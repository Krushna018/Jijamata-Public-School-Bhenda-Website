Hostel Facilities

A safe, comfortable, and disciplined home away from home for our students

Hostel Overview

Quality residential facilities designed for student comfort and academic success

Comfortable Rooms

Spacious, well-ventilated rooms with personal study desks, storage wardrobes, and clean beds for every resident student.

Hygienic Dining & Mess

Four nutritious and freshly prepared vegetarian meals served daily under strict hygienic supervision.

24/7 Security & Supervision

Round-the-clock security personnel, CCTV surveillance, and resident hostel wardens ensuring student safety.

Guided Study Hours

Dedicated morning and evening study sessions supervised by resident teachers for homework and exam preparation.

Medical Care & First Aid

First aid facilities on premises with on-call doctor availability and emergency medical assistance.

Recreation & Sports

Indoor games room, TV lounge, and evening playground sports sessions for healthy physical activity.

Hostel Daily Routine

Structured daily schedule fostering discipline, academic progress, and healthy habits

Time Activity Details
06:00 AM – 06:30 AM Morning Wake Up & Exercise Physical fitness, yoga, and fresh air exercises
06:30 AM – 07:30 AM Personal Hygiene & Cleanliness Bathing, uniform preparation, and room tidying
07:30 AM – 08:15 AM Healthy Breakfast Nutritious breakfast served in dining hall
08:30 AM – 02:30 PM School Hours Attending regular school classes and activities
02:30 PM – 03:30 PM Lunch & Rest Balanced lunch followed by short rest period
03:30 PM – 05:00 PM Supervised Evening Study Guided homework completion and teacher assistance
05:00 PM – 06:30 PM Sports & Recreation Outdoor playground games and indoor activities
06:30 PM – 07:30 PM Evening Snacks & Free Time Snacks, relaxation, and wash up
07:30 PM – 09:00 PM Night Study Session Quiet self-study and revision time
09:00 PM – 09:45 PM Dinner Hot vegetarian dinner served in mess hall
10:00 PM Lights Out Night roll call and rest time

Hostel Rules & Guidelines

  • Punctuality must be observed for all daily schedule timings.
  • Visitors/Parents allowed only during designated visiting hours.
  • Mobile phones and electronic gadgets regulated per hostel policy.
  • Cleanliness of rooms and personal belongings is mandatory.
  • Warden permission required before leaving hostel premises.
