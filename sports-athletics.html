Sports & Athletics

Building Fitness, Teamwork & Discipline

About the Activity

Physical education and sports play an important role in the overall development of students. Participation in sports helps children build fitness, teamwork, discipline, confidence and leadership skills.

Activities & Participation

Students can explore age-appropriate opportunities such as:

Athletics

Individual activities that encourage movement, fitness and perseverance.

Outdoor Games

Active games that support coordination, teamwork and participation.

Indoor Games

Indoor activities that develop focus, planning and friendly competition.

Team Sports

Shared play that helps students practise cooperation and responsibility.

Individual Sports

Activities that help children build confidence and personal discipline.

Fitness Activities

Simple physical activities that encourage healthy and active habits.
