Cultural Events

Expression, Tradition & Confidence

About the Activity

Cultural activities provide students with opportunities to express their creativity, develop confidence and appreciate India's diverse traditions and celebrations.

Activities & Participation

Students may participate in school programmes and experiences such as:

Annual Gathering

A shared school celebration that encourages participation and expression.

Cultural Programmes

Opportunities to present stories, traditions and creative performances.

National Celebrations

Activities that help students understand the importance of national occasions.

Traditional Events

Celebrations that help children appreciate India's varied heritage.

Music & Dance

Creative forms of expression that build confidence and enjoyment.

Elocution & Public Speaking

Practice in speaking clearly, sharing ideas and listening respectfully.
