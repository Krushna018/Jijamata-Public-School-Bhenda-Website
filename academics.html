Examination System

A fair and comprehensive evaluation system that tracks every student's progress

Continuous Assessment

Regular class tests, oral assessments, projects, and activities throughout the term to track student progress.

Unit Tests

Periodic unit tests conducted after completion of each unit to reinforce learning and assess understanding.

Half-Yearly & Annual Exams

Formal half-yearly and annual examinations covering the complete syllabus for all classes.

Practical & Activity-Based Exams

Practical assessments in Science, Computer Education, Art, and Physical Education for relevant classes.
